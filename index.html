Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 01-04-2025

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our app to access services such as data, airtime, bill payments, and audio sermons.

1. Information We Collect

  • Personal Info: Name, phone number, or email (for login or support)
  • Device Info: OS version, device type, unique identifiers
  • Transaction Data: Wallet usage, amounts, and service type

2. Audio Content and Sermons

The app offers audio sermons and spiritual teachings for inspiration and education. All content is:

  • Owned or licensed by the developer, or
  • Freely available for public listening, or
  • Uploaded with permission from original speakers or ministries.

Audio is streamed only and not downloadable unless explicitly permitted. No personal data is collected while listening.

3. How We Use Your Information

We allow third-party companies to serve ads and collect certain anonymous information when you visit our app. These companies may use anonymous information such as your Google Advertising ID, your device type and version, browsing activity, location and other technical data relating to your device, in order to provide advertisements

We also use your information to:

  • Enable app features
  • Process wallet transactions
  • Show personalized content
  • Monitor service usage
  • Provide customer support

4. Sharing of Information

Images entered by the user are sent to the app server in order to be retrieved later by the user himself, and so that the app can offer the functionalities according to its description

Your data is never sold or rented. We may share non-sensitive data:

  • With trusted service providers (e.g., payment APIs)
  • If legally required
  • To enforce our rights or prevent abuse

5. Data Security

We use secure protocols to protect your data. However, no system is 100% immune to breaches.

6. App Permissions

We may request:

  • Internet: For live content, services, and payments
  • Network status: To check online connectivity
  • Storage: To cache non-sensitive data

No access to SMS, contacts, or camera is required.

7. Age Restrictions

This app is intended for users aged 16 and above. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Policy Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Your continued use of the app after any change indicates your acceptance.
